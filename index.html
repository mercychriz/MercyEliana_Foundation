Our Programs

Women's Rights Protection

We work tirelessly to protect and advocate for the rights of women and young girls in our communities.

Gender-Based Violence Prevention

Through education and advocacy, we fight against all forms of gender-based violence.

Health Education

We provide education on sexually transmitted diseases, HIV/AIDS, and reproductive health issues.

Substance Abuse Prevention

Our programs help prevent drug abuse among women and young people.

Tech Training for Black Women

We provide training in technology and artificial intelligence to empower Black women with valuable skills for the digital economy.
